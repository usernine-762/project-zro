Cooked in GTA 6

A personal tool for the owner of the Cooked in GTA 6 accounts. It sends already-edited GTA 6 clips to the owner's own TikTok inbox, as drafts. It does not publish videos, sell data, or post to anyone else's account.

This is not a public posting service and not a TikTok, Rockstar, or streamer-affiliated product. After a clip is delivered to the inbox, the account owner opens the TikTok notification, reviews the video, edits the caption if needed, and posts it from the TikTok app.

How it works

  1. The owner edits a vertical clip on their own computer.
  2. They sign in with the TikTok account they own, using TikTok Login Kit, and grant permission to upload a draft.
  3. The tool sends that video to TikTok's official inbox / draft upload endpoint.
  4. TikTok notifies the owner in their inbox that a draft is ready.
  5. The owner opens the notification in the TikTok app, checks the video and caption, and decides whether to post it.

What this tool does not do

  • It does not publish or schedule posts on its own.
  • It does not upload to any TikTok account except the one that signed in.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • It does not collect data from other TikTok users.

TikTok connection

To connect the owner's TikTok account, run the local tool and complete TikTok's authorization page. If you were sent here after signing in, the one-time authorization code is shown on the callback page.
