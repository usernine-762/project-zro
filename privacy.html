Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026. This policy describes a personal tool used by the owner of the Cooked in GTA 6 accounts. It is not a multi-user product and does not sell data.

Who this applies to

The only TikTok user whose data this tool handles is the account owner who signs in, currently expected to be @cookedingta6. It does not sign in as, scrape, or store other people's TikTok accounts.

Data we receive from TikTok

When the owner authorizes the app, TikTok may provide:

  • A unique user identifier (open_id)
  • Display name and avatar, from the user.info.basic scope, used only to confirm which account connected
  • Access and refresh tokens, used only to send videos to that account's TikTok inbox and to check upload status

The tool also handles video files and captions the owner already created, solely to upload them as drafts.

How that data is used

  • To confirm the connected TikTok account belongs to the owner
  • To upload the owner's own clips to that account's inbox / drafts through TikTok's Content Posting API
  • To refresh the login when the short-lived access token expires
  • To check whether a draft was delivered to the inbox

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data.
  • We do not use the data for advertising, profiling, or analytics products.
  • We do not share tokens or videos with third parties, except TikTok as needed to complete the upload the owner started.
  • We do not publish videos automatically. Posting stays in the owner's control inside the TikTok app.

Where data is stored

Tokens and upload records are stored only on the owner's own machine, in a local file that is not published with this website. This public site does not keep a user database.

How long data is kept

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the app or the refresh token expires (TikTok currently issues refresh tokens for about 180 days). The owner can delete the local token file at any time. Videos are sent to TikTok for the draft upload and are not kept on this website.

How to revoke access

In the TikTok app or on tiktok.com, open Settings, then Security and permissions (or the equivalent Manage app permissions screen), and revoke access for Cooked in GTA 6. You can also ask us to delete the locally stored tokens by emailing the address below.

Children

This tool is not directed at children under 13, and it is not meant to be used with a TikTok account belonging to anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above will change and the new version will be posted on this page.
